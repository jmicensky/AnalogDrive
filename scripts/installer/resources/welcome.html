Welcome to the Analog Drive beta

Thanks for testing! This is a pre-release build, so please report anything that seems off.

This installs Analog Drive for every DAW on this Mac:

  • VST3 for Ableton Live and most other DAWs
  • Audio Unit for Logic Pro and GarageBand
  • Standalone app in your Applications folder

Click Customize on the Installation Type step if you only want some of these.

When it finishes, open your DAW. In Ableton Live you may need to rescan: Settings → Plug-Ins → Rescan. Logic finds it automatically.

If your DAW was already open, quit and reopen it.
